Built for daily field work

The KTS-442UT combines a bright telescope, fast reflectorless distance measurement and a dual-side display, so crews can work quickly from either face of the instrument. Its sealed body keeps dust and rain out on long survey days.

Onboard programs cover setting out, resection, area and remote height measurement, and data transfers by USB, so field notes move straight into your office software.

Key features

  • Reflectorless measurement up to 500 m
  • Dual-side graphic display and keyboard
  • Setting out, resection, area and remote height programs
  • IP66 sealed, long-life battery
  • USB data transfer and large internal memory
