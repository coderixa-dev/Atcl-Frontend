About the role

You will be the link between our customers and our product range. You listen to what a laboratory or factory needs, recommend the right equipment, and see the order through from the first inquiry to a signed quotation.

This role suits someone who enjoys technical conversations and building long-term relationships with buyers. You will work closely with our application specialists and service engineers, so every recommendation you make is backed by real product knowledge.

What you will do

  • Handle product inquiries and quotation requests that arrive through the website, phone and email
  • Visit customers, understand their testing or production needs and suggest suitable equipment
  • Prepare clear quotations and follow up until the customer decides
  • Give product demonstrations together with our application team
  • Keep customer records and the sales pipeline up to date
  • Share market feedback with the team so we can improve our product range

What we are looking for

Required
  • Bachelor's degree in engineering, science or business
  • 2 to 4 years of B2B or technical sales experience
  • Clear spoken and written English and Bangla
  • Willing to travel within Bangladesh when needed
An advantage
  • Experience selling laboratory, textile or industrial equipment
  • Existing contacts in labs, factories or universities
  • Comfortable using CRM tools and spreadsheets
  • Basic knowledge of tendering and import procedures

What you get

Commission on top of salary
Paid on every closed quotation.
Training from our brands
Product training from our partner manufacturers.
Two festival bonuses
Every year, along with your salary.
Health insurance
Covers you and your immediate family.

How we hire

  1. You apply

    Send your CV with the form below. We read every application and reply within 5 working days.

  2. Phone call

    A short chat with our HR team about your experience and what you are looking for.

  3. Interview with the sales team

    You meet the Head of Sales and talk through a real customer case.

  4. Offer

    If it is a match, you receive a written offer and a start date.
