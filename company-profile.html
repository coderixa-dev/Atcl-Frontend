Overview

Who we are, in brief

ATCL Solutions helps factories, hospitals, laboratories and research centers choose, install and maintain the right equipment.

Our eight specialized divisions cover textile, survey, software, leather, analytical, healthcare, life science and project solutions. Each one is led by engineers who understand both the technology and the customer's need.
