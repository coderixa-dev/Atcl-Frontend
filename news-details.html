We Launched Our New Online Product Catalog & Quotation Portal

05 Oct 2026 By Admin 5 min read
  1. Home
  2. News & Events
  3. Article

Finding the right equipment should not take days of phone calls and emails. Today we are proud to launch our new website with a complete online product catalog and a built-in quotation system.

For years our customers have relied on our team to help them choose laboratory, industrial and testing equipment. The new portal brings that same expertise online, so you can explore, compare and request prices whenever it suits you.

What is new on the website

We rebuilt the experience from the ground up, with speed and clarity as our main goals. Everything is organised so you can reach the product you need in just a few clicks.

Key highlights
  • A catalog of 1,000+ products with categories, brands and search
  • Add products to an inquiry list and send one combined request
  • Product brochures and specifications available to download
  • Fully responsive design that works on phones and tablets

How the inquiry system works

Browse the catalog, add the items you are interested in, and submit your details. Our sales team receives the full list and replies with a clear quotation, usually within one working day.

Our goal is simple: make it easy for every customer to find the right solution and get a fast, honest answer from real people.

Imran Hasan Managing Director

What happens next

This is only the first step. Over the coming months we will keep adding new products, brands and resources, along with service request forms and event registration.

We would love to hear what you think. If you have suggestions or questions about the new website, please get in touch with our team.
